# Project Brief: The Jawed Habib Salon – Lalganj

## 1. Executive Summary
**The Jawed Habib Salon – Lalganj** is an enterprise-grade, luxury salon digital platform designed to bridge world-class hair artistry and beauty care with the regional clientele of Lalganj and the greater Vaishali district in Bihar, India.

The platform provides:
- A high-fashion, luxury public salon website showcasing services, styling expertise, and client transformations.
- A seamless, friction-free appointment reservation flow with instant confirmation.
- A customer loyalty and membership management portal.
- An end-to-end admin command center managing chair allocations, staff schedules, pricing, service catalogs, inventory, and revenue analytics.

## 3. Brand Identity & Design System
- **Luxury Black (`#111111`)**: Evoking exclusivity, premium depth, and editorial elegance.
- **Champagne Gold (`#C6A56B`)**: Symbol of royalty, precision, and beauty artistry.
- **Ivory (`#F7F4EE`)**: Warm, soothing background and text contrast.
- **White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Clean typography and crisp visual hierarchy.
- **Muted Grey (`#777777` / `#8E8E93`)**: Subtle metadata and border treatment.

## 4. Key Target Audiences
1. **Local Residents & Families**: Seeking dependable, hygienic haircutting, hair spa, and grooming services.
2. **Brides & Grooms**: Seeking full wedding makeover packages, pre-bridal glow rituals, and bespoke event styling.
3. **Young Professionals & Students**: Seeking modern trending cuts, balayage, hair color, and beard grooming.
4. **Salon Management & Stylists**: Managing appointments, walk-ins, daily cash/UPI sales, customer records, and shift schedules.
