# Customer Booking & Portal Guide: The Jawed Habib Salon – Lalganj

Welcome to **The Jawed Habib Salon – Lalganj**. This guide explains how customers can easily book salon appointments, manage upcoming sessions, and utilize loyalty rewards.

---

## 1. How to Book an Appointment Online

### Step 1: Access the Booking Portal
Visit `https://thejawedhabib-lalganj.com/booking` or click the gold **Book Appointment** button on any page.

### Step 2: Choose Service Category & Services
1. Select your desired category:
   - **Hair Services**: Precision Haircut, Hair Spa, Keratin, Hair Coloring, Straightening.
   - **Beauty Services**: 24K Gold Facial, D-Tan Brightening, Deep Pore Cleanup.
   - **Makeup Services**: Bridal HD Makeup, Party & Occasion Makeup.
   - **Men's Grooming**: Executive Haircut, Beard Architecture, Scalp Massage.
2. Select one or more services. You can combine multiple treatments (e.g., *Haircut* + *Hair Spa*). The system automatically calculates your total session duration and estimated price.

### Step 3: Select Preferred Stylist (Optional)
You can choose a specific master stylist or select **"No Preference / First Available"** for maximum slot flexibility.

### Step 4: Pick Date & Time Slot
1. Select your preferred date (up to 30 days in advance).
2. The system dynamically displays 30-minute time slots strictly between **10:00 AM and 10:00 PM** (open all 7 days of the week).
3. If a slot is grayed out or unavailable, that time is already reserved by another customer.

### Step 5: Enter Contact Details
Provide your:
- Full Name
- 10-digit Indian Mobile Number (e.g. `9831722983`)
- Email Address (Optional for booking confirmations)
- Any special requests (e.g., hair length notes, scalp sensitivity)

### Step 6: Apply Promo Code & Review Order
If you have a discount coupon code (such as `WELCOME100`), enter it in the coupon box and click **Apply**. Review your subtotal, discount, and estimated total amount.

### Step 7: Confirm Booking
Submit your booking. You will instantly receive a unique booking confirmation reference (e.g., `JH-LLG-98421`). Status begins as `PENDING` and is confirmed by the salon reception.

## 2. Customer Account & Loyalty Points Portal

### Signing Up & Welcome Bonus (`/register`)
- Register with your name, 10-digit mobile number, and password.
- You automatically receive **100 Welcome Habib Reward Points** in your account.

### Logging In (`/login`)
- Log in anytime using your registered phone number or email and password.

### Viewing Appointments & Rescheduling (`/bookings`)
- In your client dashboard, view **Upcoming** and **Past** salon appointments.
- **Reschedule**: Need to change your time? Click **Reschedule** at least 2 hours before your appointment to choose a new available time.
- **Cancel**: You can cancel an appointment online up to 2 hours prior to the scheduled slot. For cancellations within 2 hours, please call our reception directly at `+91 7707000463`.

### Earning & Redeeming Loyalty Points (`/account`)
- Earn **1 Reward Point for every ₹10 spent** on completed salon visits.
- Points can be redeemed for discounts on hair spa treatments, facials, and premium hair products on your next visit to the salon.
