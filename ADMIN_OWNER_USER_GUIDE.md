## Quick Reference Summary

### Core Modules Overview
1. **Dashboard (`/admin/dashboard`):** Real-time KPIs for bookings, revenue, and daily client arrivals.
2. **Bookings Hub (`/admin/bookings`):** Search, check-in, start service, complete service, and collect counter balances.
3. **Calendar (`/admin/calendar`):** Visual chair capacity, 30-min slot management, and holiday blackouts.
4. **Customer CRM (`/admin/customers`):** Client contact directory, visit histories, and preferences.
5. **Services & Pricing (`/admin/services`):** Haircut, facial, spa, and bridal service rates.
6. **Payments Ledger (`/admin/payments`):** Razorpay online advances and offline cash/UPI receipts.
7. **Media & Before/After (`/admin/media`):** Paired transformation comparisons and salon photography.
8. **CMS & Announcements (`/admin/content`):** Website top announcement bar, hero text, and promotional banners.
9. **Reports & Exports (`/admin/reports`):** CSV downloads for accounting and customer marketing.
10. **Settings (`/admin/settings`):** Salon hours, business coordinates, and user accounts.

### Daily Checklists
- **Morning (10:00 AM):** Review today's bookings, verify stylist rosters, inspect notifications.
- **Evening (10:00 PM):** Complete active visits, tally cash/UPI collections, log out of shared terminals.
