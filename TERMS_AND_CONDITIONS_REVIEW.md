## 3. Strict Compliance Guardrails Enforced

1. **No Invented Cancellation Windows:** The terms do not invent commercial forfeiture percentages (e.g., "50% non-refundable deposit"). Instead, commercial deposit treatment is referred to the Cancellation and Refund Policy with concierge review.
2. **No Misleading Guarantees:** Statements regarding hair transformations explicitly note that results depend on individual hair geometry, history, and density.
3. **Canonical Route Synchronization:** Both `/terms-and-conditions` and legacy `/terms` are live, with `/terms` carrying a canonical tag pointing to `/terms-and-conditions` to maintain SEO integrity.
