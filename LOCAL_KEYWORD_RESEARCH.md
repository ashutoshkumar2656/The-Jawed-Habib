## 1. Local Search Ecosystem & Customer Intent Analysis

Search intent for salon services in tier-2/3 regional hubs like Lalganj and the wider Vaishali district falls into four distinct categories:

1. **High Commercial Intent ("Near Me" & Local Discovery):**  
   Users actively seeking an immediate haircut, beauty treatment, or salon visit. They search by location and landmark (e.g. *Tinpulwa Chowk*, *Lalganj*).
2. **Event & High-Ticket Transactional Intent (Bridal, Wedding, Party):**  
   Brides, grooms, and families looking for specialized makeover artists, portfolio samples, and transparent package pricing weeks or months before ceremonies.
3. **Brand Authority Intent:**  
   Customers specifically seeking the official *Jawed Habib* franchise in Lalganj to avoid uncertified or imitation parlours.
4. **Service & Treatment Informational Intent:**  
   Customers investigating premium treatments like *Keratin*, *Hair Spa*, *Botox*, or *Hydra Facials* to determine pricing, safety, and suitability.
